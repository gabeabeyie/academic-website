# Design rationale

This design gives the homepage one job: introduce the researcher and direct
visitors to the research, CV, teaching, and contact pages. Papers have a dedicated
catalogue so the homepage can stay concise as the research portfolio grows.

## Reference sites reviewed

- [Kurt Mitman](https://www.kurtmitman.com/): concise homepage and separate research catalogue.
- [Emi Nakamura](https://eminakamura.com/): descriptive research introduction, category jumps, abstracts, and replication links.
- [Stefanie Stantcheva](https://www.stefanie-stantcheva.com/): distinct destinations for academic and broader audiences.
- [Pascal Michaillat](https://pascalmichaillat.org/): compact personal introduction and clear destination links.
- [Benjamin Moll](https://benjaminmoll.com/): a clearly stated research agenda and separate papers, lectures, and code.
- [Jonathan Roth](https://www.jonathandroth.com/): recognizable academic identity and direct CV and paper navigation.
- [Paul Goldsmith-Pinkham](https://paulgp.com/): practical access to research, CV, and teaching materials.
- [Gabriel Zucman](https://gabriel-zucman.eu/): immediately accessible research and data links.
- [Jacob Steinhardt](https://jsteinhardt.stat.berkeley.edu/): plain-language explanation of research questions.
- [Matthew Gentzkow](https://matthewgentzkow.com/): concise introduction with separate research and data destinations.

Content and structure were reviewed across all ten. Visual inspection was also
possible for the Mitman, Stantcheva, and Michaillat homepages. The Stantcheva page
rendered incompletely in the review browser, so it was not used as a typography
reference. The design is original; no site's source code, artwork, or prose was copied.

## Choices for Gabriel

- Four real pages: Home, Research, Teaching, Contact; CV stays directly linked.
- White background, deep navy text, and restrained blue links.
- Real portrait, short first-person introduction, and three research themes.
- Publication details and abstracts remain directly accessible.
- Course records are expandable; students have a visible appointment link.
- System fonts and native HTML controls keep the site lightweight and maintainable.
- No invented metrics, decorative data charts, or generic academic-profile links.
